OBSERVED IN PRACTICE

The problem was repeated work—not a lack of data.

During my work in the Mayor’s Office of Code Enforcement, I observed the surrounding operational workflow for property research. A property could return through enforcement after an earlier case had closed, and useful research from the prior case was not always carried forward as a reusable starting point.

I was not a title researcher. My perspective came from working inside the broader operational environment and seeing how complex information, handoffs, financial and administrative processes, and case activity intersected.

The design opportunity became: research the property history once, preserve it, monitor what changes, and restart only when the evidence requires it.

HUMAN + AI WORKFLOW

AI does the searching. People make the consequential call.

ParcelTrace is designed around augmentation rather than autonomous legal or title decisions.

01

AI assembles

Retrieve prior research, ingest authorized records, extract entities, and compare systems.

02

AI explains

Show what changed, what agrees, what conflicts, confidence levels, and the source evidence.

03

Researcher verifies

Resolve identity, succession or heir issues, handwriting, parcel ambiguity, and other exceptions.

04

Reviewer approves

Confirm the research is sufficient for the next operational step and preserve a versioned audit trail.

AI assistance+Human accountability

Repetitive search and comparison can be accelerated. Ambiguity, sufficiency, and consequential decisions stay with people.

CONTEXT LAYER

Market context belongs beside the case—not inside the title conclusion.

ParcelTrace could pair an official MVA benchmark with a separately labeled, refreshable market-context layer built from authorized sources. Potential signals include permit activity, recorded sales where permitted, owner occupancy, vacancy/distress, code activity, and tax signals.

FUTURE VALIDATION

What I would test before implementation

  • Can researchers distinguish “AI found no material change” from “legal title is unchanged”?
  • Which conflicts should trigger targeted research versus full manual review?
  • What confidence thresholds should force human verification?
  • Does source-image + transcription review reduce effort without increasing errors?
  • Can entity matching surface likely variants without encouraging unsafe merges?
  • Which users actually benefit from market context: researchers, supervisors, or another role?

DESIGN TAKEAWAY

The best AI workflow is not the one with the fewest people.

It is the one that gives people better evidence, clearer uncertainty, less repetitive work, and an explicit place to exercise judgment.

ParcelTrace reframes the problem from “automate title research” to “preserve institutional knowledge and focus human expertise where the evidence changes.”
