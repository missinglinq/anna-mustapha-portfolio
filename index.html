A SYSTEM, NOT A SINGLE USE CASE

Property history shouldn’t disappear when a case—or transaction—ends.

ParcelTrace began with a municipal code-enforcement workflow: prior title research can lose practical value when a case closes and later reopens.

The same design question may extend to lending and property transactions: what if a verified property baseline persisted, and professionals focused on what changed?

Future-market hypothesis only. ParcelTrace does not claim to replace legal title examination, title insurance, or professional judgment.

MY STORY

Experience inside complex systems changed how I see design.

After more than a decade in public-sector operations, finance, data, and cross-functional decision-making, I became interested in the space where AI can remove repetitive work without removing human accountability.

I’m bringing that systems perspective into product and UX design: understand the evidence, find the friction, and make the next decision clearer.
