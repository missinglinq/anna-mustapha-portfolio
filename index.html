THE DESIGN QUESTION

What becomes possible when a system remembers what the organization already knows?

ParcelTrace grew from an operational problem I observed while working in the Mayor’s Office of Code Enforcement: useful property research could be repeated when a property returned through an enforcement workflow.

I was not a title researcher. I translated that broader operational observation into a product question: could a persistent, verified baseline help professionals focus on what changed instead of repeatedly starting from zero?

HUMAN + AI WORKFLOW

AI assists. People remain accountable.

A deliberately bounded interaction model: automate repetition, expose uncertainty, preserve human judgment.

1

Assemble

Retrieve prior work and ingest authorized records.

2

Explain

Surface changes, confidence, conflicts, and evidence.

3

Verify

Researchers investigate ambiguity and exceptions.

4

Approve

People make the consequential call and preserve the decision.

DESIGNED LIKE A PRODUCT

Dense information, without visual overload.

The interface treats property intelligence as a working environment, not a single answer. Filters, change states, confidence indicators, linked evidence, and review routes help users narrow the field while keeping the system legible.
