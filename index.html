MY STORY

Curiosity became a career direction.

I’ve always been drawn to solving problems, helping people, art, and technology. After more than a decade working inside complex government operations, my curiosity about AI and design became something I wanted to build with — not simply watch from the sidelines.

Today I’m applying that analytical background to human-centered products: understanding the system, finding the friction, and designing a clearer way through it.
