THE DESIGN QUESTION

What becomes possible when a system remembers what the organization already knows?

ParcelTrace grew from an operational problem I observed in the Mayor’s Office of Code Enforcement: useful property research could be repeated when a property returned through an enforcement workflow.

I translated that broader observation into a product hypothesis: preserve a verified baseline, then help professionals investigate what changed instead of repeatedly starting from zero.
