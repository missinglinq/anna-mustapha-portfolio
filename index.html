A BIGGER QUESTION

What becomes possible when we remember what we already know?

ParcelTrace started with a real operational problem: useful property research can lose practical value when a case ends and later returns.

The concept asks whether a persistent, verified property baseline could help professionals focus on what changed instead of repeatedly starting from zero.
